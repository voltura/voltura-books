# Voltura Books 0.1.1

Read while you browse, and get back to your books faster.

## What's new

- Read PDF, EPUB, Word, RTF, text, and HTML files directly in **Browse books**.
- Enjoy a new fullscreen reader for more comfortable reading. Use **F11** to enter or leave fullscreen, or **Esc** to return to Browse books.
- Turn pages using the controls at the sides of the reader or the arrow keys.
- Keep your place when resizing the window or switching between the normal and fullscreen reader.
- View JPEG, PNG, GIF, and BMP images in fullscreen.
- Open large text files without the previous 8 MB reading limit.
- See clear progress messages while documents and pages are loading.
- Open **Browse books** directly from its new Start menu shortcut.
- Return quickly to your books with **Browse folder**, which now remembers the folder you last selected.
- Clear searches with one click and sort books by name or date modified.
- Navigate more easily with clearer keyboard focus, more consistent controls, themed scrollbars, and improved high-contrast support.
- Avoid duplicate EPUB cover pages when the cover is already shown in Browse books.
- Try the sending process safely with the clearer **Don't send emails** and **Test sending** options. Testing does not send an email or change your sent-book history.
- Use **Open with default app** whenever you prefer another reader or the built-in reader cannot open a book.

Your reading stays on your PC, and reading or previewing a book never sends an email.

## Install

Download **VolturaBooks-Setup-0.1.1-win-x64.exe** and run it. Then enter your Kindle and sender email addresses in Settings and approve the sender address in your Amazon account.

Voltura Books is designed and tested for Windows 11 x64. This release is unsigned, so Windows may show a publisher warning.

## Sending books

Voltura Books can send EPUB, PDF, Word, RTF, text, HTML, and supported image files up to 50 MB. Amazon handles conversion and delivery, which may take a few minutes after the email has been accepted.
