# Voltura Books 0.1.3

Browse larger book collections more smoothly, including books stored in subfolders and online-only OneDrive files.

## What's new

- Turn on **Include subfolders** to find supported books, documents, and images throughout the selected folder.
- Start browsing immediately while books from subfolders continue appearing in the background.
- See folder names alongside nested files, making books with the same filename easier to distinguish.
- Browse online-only OneDrive files without downloading the entire collection.
- Select **Download and view** when you want to preview an online-only file.
- Enjoy smoother list and tile selection in large collections, with up to 100 files ready to send at once.
- Benefit from a cleaner Browse books layout and clearer progress feedback.

Your reading stays on your PC, and browsing, reading, or previewing a book never sends an email.

## Install

Download **VolturaBooks-Setup-0.1.3-win-x64.exe** and run it. Then enter your Kindle and sender email addresses in Settings and approve the sender address in your Amazon account.

Voltura Books is designed and tested for Windows 11 x64. This release is unsigned, so Windows may show a publisher warning.

## Sending books

Voltura Books can send EPUB, PDF, RTF, TXT, HTML/HTM, DOC/DOCX, JPEG/JPG, PNG, GIF, and BMP files up to 50 MB. Amazon handles conversion and delivery, which may take a few minutes after the email has been accepted.
