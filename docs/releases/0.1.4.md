# Voltura Books 0.1.4

Navigate to settings and view about info more simple.

## What's new

- Added **About** button to settings view.
- Added **Settings** button to about view.

Your reading stays on your PC, and browsing, reading, or previewing a book never sends an email.

## Install

Download **VolturaBooks-Setup-0.1.4-win-x64.exe** and run it. Then enter your Kindle and sender email addresses in Settings and approve the sender address in your Amazon account.

Voltura Books is designed and tested for Windows 11 x64. This release is unsigned, so Windows may show a publisher warning.

## Sending books

Voltura Books can send EPUB, PDF, RTF, TXT, HTML/HTM, DOC/DOCX, JPEG/JPG, PNG, GIF, and BMP files up to 50 MB. Amazon handles conversion and delivery, which may take a few minutes after the email has been accepted.
